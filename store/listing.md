**Full description**
Tastemate recommends restaurants, bars, museums and shops in London using people who rate places the way you do, instead of the crowd average.

For diners (always free)
• Rate places you know, from Dishoom to the National Theatre, or import your Google, Tripadvisor or Yelp reviews.
• Tastemate finds your 10 closest taste matches and predicts your score for places you haven't tried.
• See your matches' ratings next to yours, on a live Google map, with walking directions in one tap.
• Claim live offers from nearby places. Each voucher has a QR code the venue scans when you visit.

For businesses
• Send time-limited offers, such as 2-for-1 or 50% off, to the first people nearby who are likely to enjoy your place.
• Scan customers' vouchers with your phone camera to record each visit and the bill.
• See uptake, conversion and spend by offer type, time of day and day of week.
• 10 free tokens every month. Extra tokens are 75p each, or 45p each when you buy 100 or more.

Beta notes
This beta covers 64 London places: restaurants, bars, museums and galleries, theatres and shows, and shops. While the community grows, recommendations also use 50 simulated profiles.
