Legal basis

We process account, rating, voucher and purchase data because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for (contract). We keep payment records because the law requires it (legal obligation).

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in (servers in the UK/EU region we choose).
  • Stripe: processes business payments and sends receipts (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Google Maps Platform: shows maps and live place ratings. When a map is shown, your device requests map data from Google (Google privacy policy).

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. There are no ads and no third-party analytics in Tastemate.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, ratings and account details straight away. For diners, venues keep anonymous visit counts (voucher code, times and bill) with no link to you. For businesses, offers, vouchers and token history are deleted. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your name at the top, then Delete my account. On the web, go to APP_URL/#account, sign in, and choose Delete my account. You can also email CONTACT_EMAIL.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing CONTACT_EMAIL. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Simulated profiles

While the community grows, recommendations also use 50 simulated profiles. They are not real people.

Children

Tastemate is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes
